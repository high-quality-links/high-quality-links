The Importance of Regular HVAC Maintenance

Routine maintenance can significantly extend the life of your HVAC system while optimizing its performance. According to the U.S. Department of Energy, regular maintenance can improve the efficiency of HVAC units by up to 30%. That can lead to substantial savings on energy bills. Several key tasks include:

  • Checking and replacing air filters regularly
  • Cleaning condenser and evaporator coils
  • Inspecting ductwork for leaks
  • Ensuring refrigerant levels are optimal
  • Testing thermostats for accuracy
