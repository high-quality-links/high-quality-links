Understanding the Pathways to US Permanent Residency

US permanent residency can be obtained primarily through three major pathways:

  • Family Sponsorship: Individuals can apply if they have relatives in the U.S. who can sponsor them, typically spouses, parents, or children.
  • Employment-Based Visas: Many companies sponsor foreign workers to fill specialized positions, allowing them to secure permanent residency.
  • Investment (EB-5 Program): By investing a substantial amount in U.S. businesses, investors can gain residency, benefiting both the U.S. economy and their prospects for a new life.
